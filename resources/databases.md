# Bioinformatics Databases

## Nucleotide Databases

- **NCBI GenBank** - Comprehensive public database of nucleotide sequences
- **EMBL-EBI** - European nucleotide sequence database
- **DDBJ** - DNA Data Bank of Japan

## Protein Databases

- **UniProt** - Universal Protein Resource (Swiss-Prot + TrEMBL)
- **Pfam** - Protein family database
- **InterPro** - Protein families, domains, and functional sites

## Structure Databases

- **PDB (Protein Data Bank)** - 3D structure data of biological macromolecules
- **SCOP** - Structural Classification of Proteins
- **CATH** - Protein structure classification database

## Literature Databases

- **PubMed** - Biomedical literature database
- **PubMed Central** - Free full-text archive

## Specialized Databases

- **KEGG** - Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes
- **STRING** - Protein-protein interaction database
- **Gene Ontology (GO)** - Gene functional annotation
